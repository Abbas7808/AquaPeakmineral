Our 9-Step Purification Process

At Aqua Water, quality isn't an afterthought. Every single drop passes through 9 controlled purification, disinfection, and mineral-enrichment stages before bottling.

01
Collection Stage

Careful Water Collection

Protected-source water moves through food-grade, sanitized hygienic pipelines directly into the automated purification infrastructure.

Source Safety Verified Stage
02
Pre-Filter Stage

Pre-Filtration (Sediment & Sand)

High-efficiency multi-layer sediment and silica sand filtration remove suspended physical particles, silt, dust, mud and rust.

Particulate Barrier Verified Stage
03
Carbon Stage

Activated Carbon Filtration

Virgin granular activated carbon absorbs chlorine, volatile organic compounds (VOCs), eliminates odors and enhances natural crispness.

Odor & Taste Polish Verified Stage
04
Reduction Stage

Contaminant & Hardness Reduction

Dedicated filtration stages reduce heavy mineral loads, iron, and manganese, protecting membrane integrity and water softness.

Hardness Control Verified Stage
05
RO Stage Crucial Quality Barrier

Reverse Osmosis (RO) Purification

Advanced semi-permeable membranes filter at 0.0001 microns, removing 99%+ of dissolved solids, heavy metals, micro-plastics and toxins.

WHO Standard Membrane Certified
06
Storage Stage

Food-Grade Stainless Steel Storage

Purified water is retained in sealed food-grade 304/316 stainless steel buffer tanks with positive-pressure sterile air locks preventing recontamination.

Hygienic Buffer Verified Stage
07
Minerals Stage Crucial Quality Barrier

Mineral Balancing & Remineralization

Essential electrolytes (Calcium, Magnesium, Potassium) are scientifically dosed to achieve ideal drinking water TDS (~130-160 ppm) and crisp, natural palate.

PSQCA Standard Mineral Calibrated
08
Disinfection Stage Crucial Quality Barrier

Dual UV & Ozone Disinfection

Short-wave Ultraviolet (UV-C) radiation destroys micro-biological pathogen DNA, followed by micro-bubble ozone sterilization preserving bottle freshness.

Microbial Zero Biologically Pure
09
Bottling Stage

Cleanroom Bottling & Quality Seal

Automated multi-stage bottle sanitization, sterile reverse-rinse, contact-free filling, hermetic cap sealing, batch stamping and final QC inspection.

Aseptic Packaging QC Verified
