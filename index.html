01 Collection

Careful Water Collection

Protected-source water moves through hygienic pipelines into the purification system.

02 Pre-Filter

Pre-Filtration

Sediment and sand filtration remove dust, sand, mud and visible impurities.

03 Carbon

Carbon Filtration

Activated carbon improves smell, color and taste before advanced purification.

04 Reduction

Contaminant Reduction

Dedicated stages help reduce iron, manganese and other physical contaminants.

05 RO Purification

Reverse Osmosis Purification

Advanced RO helps reduce dissolved salts, heavy impurities and unwanted contaminants.

06 Storage

Food-Grade Water Storage

Purified water is stored in sanitized food-grade tanks to maintain hygiene and safety.

07 Minerals

Mineral Balancing

Essential minerals are balanced to improve taste, freshness and drinking quality.

08 UV + Ozone

UV & Ozone Disinfection

UV sterilization and ozonation help eliminate harmful microorganisms before bottling.

09 Bottling

Hygienic Bottling & Quality Check

Every bottle is filled, sealed, labelled and checked under safe drinking water practices.
